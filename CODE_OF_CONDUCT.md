# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

UND Industries wants this community to be useful, welcoming, and professional. Participate in good faith, focus criticism on ideas and products rather than people, and help keep discussions constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Give actionable feedback when possible.
- Assume good intent, but correct misinformation clearly.
- Keep discussions relevant to UND products, services, and community topics.
- Protect personal, confidential, and security-sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, targeted insults, or discriminatory abuse.
- Spam, scams, impersonation, or deliberate disruption.
- Posting private information, credentials, customer data, internal source code, or confidential company material.
- Publishing sensitive vulnerability details in public channels.
- Encouraging unauthorized access, abuse, or destructive testing.

## Moderation

UND may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates these standards or creates a security, privacy, legal, or safety risk. Repeated or severe violations may result in participation restrictions.

## Scope

This code applies to activity in this repository and its public community surfaces.
