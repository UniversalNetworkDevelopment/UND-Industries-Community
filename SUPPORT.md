# Support

This repository is a public community and feedback channel for UND Industries.

## Use a public issue for

- Reproducible product bugs
- Feature requests
- General product feedback
- Questions that can be answered without sharing private account information

## Do not use public issues for

- Passwords, tokens, recovery codes, API keys, or credentials
- Billing or account details that identify a person
- Customer records or private company information
- Security vulnerabilities or suspected sensitive-data exposure
- Internal source code or private infrastructure details

For security-sensitive reports, follow `SECURITY.md`.

## What to include

When asking for help, include the product name, platform, version if known, what you were trying to do, what happened, and the steps that reproduce the problem. Redact sensitive information before posting screenshots or logs.

UND may redirect, close, or lock reports that are duplicates, off-topic, incomplete, or unsafe to handle publicly.
